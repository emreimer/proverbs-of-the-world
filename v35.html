Emre Imer

Emre Imer calls himself a citizen of the world. He was born in Turkey, spent part of his childhood in Germany, and lived in Czechia and Japan as an exchange student. He co-founded a company in Greece and later founded his own company in Estonia. He holds a bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering and a master’s degree in Insurance, and works in international business development. As an author and editor he shares what he has learned across cultures.
